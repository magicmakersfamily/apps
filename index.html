James’s Nature Aquarium水草

Version 3.4 · Launched September 24, 2026

Start with an empty tank and build a living ecosystem in the style of Takashi Amano. Add plants, stones, shrimp, fish, light — even a turtle — then watch a week go by. Keep everything in balance to reach a Harmony score of 90.

  • Photosynthesis
  • Nitrogen cycle
  • Food webs
  • Systems thinking
  • Chinese words
  • Music
  • Ages 8 and up
  • Progress saved on your device

Character Book認字簿

Version 1.0 Beta · Launched October 12, 2026

A new picture story in Traditional Chinese every morning, made for a five-year-old who is learning to read. Read it page by page, then write the day’s characters on a grid that shows the stroke order: numbered starts, arrows for the direction, and a little 筆順 strip that builds each character one stroke at a time. Trace it, or try it on your own. Tap the words you know. It works best on a tablet in full screen (Add to Home Screen). This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Chinese reading
  • Writing
  • Stroke order
  • Daily story
  • Beta
  • Ages 4 and up (a grown-up helps at first)
  • Progress saved on your device
  • Sound none

Mid-Autumn Mayhem中秋大混亂

Version 1.7 · Launched September 25, 2026

Build the biggest Mid-Autumn party under the full moon: buildings, families, lanterns, mooncakes, tea, mahjong and fireworks. Overdo anything and the fire trucks, ambulances or police show up. Tap the Jade Rabbit and Chang’e in time, and read the moon legends behind them. Fruit brings monkeys, too much food brings a terrible smell, and lantern riddles test your Chinese. A storyteller explains everything in English and Cantonese, and a telescope lets you look up close.

  • Cause and effect
  • Ratios
  • Chinese legends
  • Cantonese narration
  • Hong Kong
  • Music
  • Ages 8 and up
  • Best score saved on your device
  • Sound optional

Blow Up大爆發

Version 1.4 Beta · Launched September 27, 2026

Live one day with Pip and watch the pressure bucket fill, drop by drop: hunger, rushing, noise, a broken cookie. When it overflows, Pip blows up (a glitter volcano, a Hangry Monster…). Then help Pip calm down, make up and learn why. A storyteller explains everything out loud, so it works for kids who can’t read yet. This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Big feelings
  • Self-regulation
  • Cause and effect
  • Storyteller voice
  • Chinese words
  • Beta
  • Ages 4 to 7, with a grown-up
  • Stickers and skills saved on your device
  • Sound on (spoken story)

Spellicious

Version 4.40 · Launched September 30, 2026

Say a word and see it appear in giant letters. The spoken word highlights in orange, and Spell mode highlights each letter or number as it is spoken. Numbers like 10 say “ten,” while 111 says “one one one.” Choose Default, Robot, Chipmunk, Storyteller, or Slow Motion voices, plus speeds from 0.5x to 2x. Tap the mic or press the space bar to start or interrupt speech.

  • Phonics
  • Reading
  • Speech
  • Syllables
  • Ages 5 and up
  • Progress nothing saved — just say a word
  • Sound on (mic and spoken words)

Ready To Go!準備出門

Version 1.4 · Launched October 2, 2026

A calm countdown for busy mornings. Add your own steps, like “brush your teeth” or “shoes on,” and pick when each one is said out loud. Each step shows as a dot on the timer ring, so kids can see what’s next. Tell it where you’re going and when you need to arrive, and the timer works out when to leave. Add your ZIP code to see the current weather and what to wear.

  • Routines
  • Telling time
  • Independence
  • Spoken reminders
  • Ages 4 and up, with a grown-up
  • Steps saved on your device
  • Sound on (spoken reminders)

Brush Buddy刷牙好夥伴

Version 2.4 Beta · Launched October 3, 2026

Two minutes of brushing, the way dentists teach it: four parts of the mouth, and in each one the outsides, the chewing tops and the insides. A friendly face looks at the teeth to brush next, a calm voice says “this side” or “the other side”, and the sugar bugs leave only from the teeth you are brushing right now. Then brush your tongue, spit without rinsing, and the face shows its sparkly smile. Grown-ups can see why these steps (based on ADA and AAPD guidance) in the Grown-ups panel. This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Healthy habits
  • Left and right
  • Routines
  • Spoken steps
  • Beta
  • Ages 3 and up
  • Settings saved on your device
  • Sound on (recorded voice and soft chimes)

Cosmic Journey宇宙之旅

Version 1.0 Beta · Launched October 17, 2026

Tap the glowing core to set off the Big Bang, then travel 13.8 billion years to today: the first stars, Earth and the Moon, the first microbes that made oxygen, the explosion of ocean life, the dinosaurs and the asteroid, and finally people and rockets. Cosmo the cosmic cat tells each chapter out loud, every chapter has a mini-game, and glowing spots hold fun facts. This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.
