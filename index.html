Cosmic Journey宇宙之旅

Travel 13.8 billion years to today.

Ages 6+ · English or 粵語

Play

Climb aboard the Time Cruiser with Cosmo and Professor Know-A-Lot. The time drive has jammed and thrown you into a wormhole, so steer through the time rings and squeeze the drive to break out (in our story, that sets off the Big Bang), then fly through real stars as you travel 13.8 billion years to today: the first star, Earth and the Moon, the tiny cells that made oxygen, the first animals to walk on land, the dinosaurs and the asteroid, and finally people and rockets. Wise Professor Know-A-Lot, an owl with glasses, tells each chapter out loud and explains the science (and says when scientists don’t know yet), while Cosmo the cosmic cat asks the questions. Every mini-game shows what you made happen (a star ignites, Earth grows, the sky clears, a rocket launches), and Cosmo asks a question to talk about together. A story line and a “universe in one year” calendar show how long ago it all was, you collect stickers, and at the end you put the whole story in order. Play it in English or in Traditional Chinese with a Cantonese voice (粵語). There is an easy and calm mode for little fingers. This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Space adventure
  • Earth history
  • Evolution
  • Time and scale
  • Mini-games
  • Spoken story
  • English or 粵語
  • Ages 6 and up
  • Progress saved on your device
  • Sound on (recorded voice, sound effects and Vivaldi’s Spring)

GUTS大肚仔歷險

Whatever Gus eats becomes motion.

Ages 5+ · keyboard, PlayStation pad or touch

Play

A comic platformer about Gus, a round little kid whose belly turns what he eats and feels into motion. This morning his breakfast bao is missing: Mimi the cat has it, so follow her through the house. Tap a fizzy drink and your belly rocket takes off, bump a laundry basket and socks fly, spin a pinwheel, or grow a bounce flower in the garden that stays for next time. The first level is playable with jumps alone; blasting is for shortcuts and secrets. Detroit and the trip around the world to Grandma’s Mid-Autumn feast come next. This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Curiosity
  • Controller play
  • Cantonese words
  • Ages 5 and up
  • Progress saved on your device
  • Sound on (original music and a spoken story)

Character Book認字簿

A new picture story in Chinese every morning.

Ages 4+

Play

A new picture story in Traditional Chinese every morning, made for a five-year-old who is learning to read. Read it page by page, then write the day’s characters on a grid that shows the stroke order: numbered starts, arrows for the direction, and a little 筆順 strip that builds each character one stroke at a time. Trace it, or try it on your own. Tap the words you know. It works best on a tablet in full screen (Add to Home Screen). This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Chinese reading
  • Writing
  • Stroke order
  • Daily story
  • Ages 4 and up (a grown-up helps at first)
  • Progress saved on your device
  • Sound none

Blow Up大爆發

Learn what happens when feelings overflow.

Ages 4–7

Play

Live one day with Pip and watch the pressure bucket fill, drop by drop: hunger, rushing, noise, a broken cookie. When it overflows, Pip blows up (a glitter volcano, a Hangry Monster…). Then help Pip calm down, make up and learn why. A storyteller explains everything out loud, so it works for kids who can’t read yet. This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Self-regulation
  • Cause and effect
  • Storyteller voice
  • Chinese words
  • Ages 4 to 7, with a grown-up
  • Stickers and skills saved on your device

Spellicious

Say a word and see it in giant letters.

Ages 5+

Play

Say a word and see it appear in giant letters. The spoken word highlights in orange, and Spell mode highlights each letter or number as it is spoken. Numbers like 10 say “ten,” while 111 says “one one one.” Choose Default, Robot, Chipmunk, Storyteller, or Slow Motion voices, plus speeds from 0.5x to 2x. Tap the mic or press the space bar to start or interrupt speech.

  • Phonics
  • Reading
  • Speech
  • Syllables
  • Ages 5 and up
  • Progress nothing saved — just say a word
  • Sound on (mic and spoken words)

Ready To Go!準備出門

Make getting ready easier.

Ages 4+

Open

Brush Buddy刷牙好夥伴

Two minutes of brushing, done right.

Ages 3+

Play

Two minutes of brushing, the way dentists teach it: four parts of the mouth, and in each one the outsides, the chewing tops and the insides. A friendly face looks at the teeth to brush next, a calm voice says “this side” or “the other side”, and the sugar bugs leave only from the teeth you are brushing right now. Then brush your tongue, spit without rinsing, and the face shows its sparkly smile. Grown-ups can see why these steps (based on ADA and AAPD guidance) in the Grown-ups panel. This game is in beta: we’re still testing it with kids and improving it.

  • Healthy habits
  • Left and right
  • Routines
  • Spoken steps
  • Ages 3 and up
  • Settings saved on your device
  • Sound on (recorded voice and soft chimes)
