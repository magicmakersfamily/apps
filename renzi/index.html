For grown-ups

A new story and its characters arrive here each morning. There are two separate kinds of practice. 認字 Recognize is for reading: tapping a character means “I know this one when I see it.” It does not mean James can say it, explain it or write it. 寫字 Write is for making the character: the stroke order, then writing it from memory. Recognizing a character before writing it is the normal order.

Nothing leaves this device.

What leaves this device. By default, nothing. Stories are only downloaded, and your taps and writing stay in this browser. If a parent has set this device up with a private link, it also sends the family’s own private log just these events: which characters were tapped as recognized, which were written, how many mistakes, and which story pages were finished. It sends no other browsing, personal or device information.

To set up a device once, use the setup link from your family log (see renzi/SETUP.md in the shelf repository). Until then nothing is sent.
