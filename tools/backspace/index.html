Mac utility · macOS only

Back Space

Back Space copies photos and videos from your iPhone to your Mac, checks that each copy matches the original, and then frees up space on the phone. Nothing is removed from the iPhone until the copy is saved and checked, and you confirm.

Download

Download Back Space (.zip)

Version 1.0. Requires a Mac, a USB cable, and Python 3 (most Macs have it).

How to install

  1. Unzip the download. You'll get a folder called “Back Space Installer”.
  2. Double-click Install Back Space.command. The first time, macOS may say it can't be opened. Right-click the file, choose Open, then click Open again.
  3. Choose the folder where your backups should go. Pictures is the default.
  4. Wait about a minute while the phone tools are set up.
  5. Open Back Space from Applications.

Using it

  1. Plug in your iPhone, unlock it, and tap Trust if asked.
  2. Open Back Space and enter how much space you want to free up, in GB.
  3. Back Space saves the oldest photos and videos to your Mac and checks each one.
  4. Review the summary, then press Free Up to remove the checked items from your iPhone.

Good to know

  • Your backups stay on your Mac, in the folder you chose. Each iPhone gets its own folder inside it.
  • Back Space checks for updates once a day and tells you when one is available. It never installs anything without you.
  • Files that can't be read are logged and skipped, so the backup can carry on.
  • To uninstall, drag Back Space from Applications to the Trash. Your backups are not touched.
