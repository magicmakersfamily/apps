How to play

Gus is a round little kid with a very expressive belly. Whatever he eats and feels becomes motion. Greens and fruit make him springy. Sweets, grease and big feelings build pressure you can blast like a rocket. A well-fed Gus blasts long and straight. A frazzled one blasts wild. Breathe to let the pressure out slowly and stay in control.

Press a button on a plugged-in or Bluetooth PlayStation controller once and the game finds it. In Gut Check mode the stick aims your blast.

Playing with one finger, a mouse, or an AI helper

In Settings turn on Tap mode (one tap keeps going for a moment), Step mode (the game waits between presses), On-screen buttons (clickable with a mouse) and Text view (describes the game in words). Add ?ai=1 to the web address to switch all four on, for an AI assistant that can press keys and click but not hold buttons.

What it teaches

  • Whole foods give steady energy; sugar gives a quick burst and then a crash.
  • Feelings fill the same belly as food, and slow breathing lets them out.
  • Eating well makes the wild stuff easier to control.
