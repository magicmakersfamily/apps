For grown-ups

A new story and its characters arrive here each morning. James reads the story, writes the characters with the stroke order shown, and taps the words he knows.

This device keeps everything to itself.

Set up a device once with the setup link from your family log (see renzi/SETUP.md in the shelf repository). Until then nothing leaves this page.
