How an AI helper (or anyone who can't hold keys) can play

The picture is drawn on a canvas, so the game also describes itself in words. Turn on Turn mode: the "Turn-by-turn mode" button on the start screen, the Turn mode button in the pause menu, the T key, or open this page with ?ai=1 at the end of the address.

In Turn mode the world freezes between moves, so there is no hurry. Each key press or button click is one move, and the text box "Turn mode" says what is around the robot after every move. Press "Show details" (or open the page with ?ai=1) for the full readout with positions, heights and an exact Hint line (id ai-panel). "Show me" draws the suggested move.

Moves: Right arrow or D = step right. Left arrow or A = step left. E = jump right. Q = jump left. Space, W or Up arrow = jump straight up. S, Down arrow or the Wait button = wait half a second (for a lift, a laser or a drone). R restarts the run from the beginning. Keys 1, 2 and 3 pick the jump size: 1 = small hop, 2 = full jump, 3 = rocket jump (a double jump, the highest and farthest).

Goal: reach the Bright Spot portal at x 7315, at the far right. Golden gears give points. Land on top of red hover-drones to bonk them; touching them from the side or touching a red laser knocks you back. Falling down a gap puts you back at the last checkpoint flag. Pink spring pads launch you very high.

For programs: window.RobotJourney.ai.state() returns the same information as an object, window.RobotJourney.ai.text() returns the text, and window.RobotJourney.ai.act('right' | 'left' | 'jumpRight' | 'jumpLeft' | 'jumpUp' | 'wait' | 'power1' | 'power2' | 'power3') makes one move.
