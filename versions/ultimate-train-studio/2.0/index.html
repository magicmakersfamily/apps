About this train

Look down at a wooden model-railway board at night. Paint the engine, snap a few chunky pieces into a small loop, and drive to the lantern festival in the corner. The windows light up and your friends step off before Ride again. Kid Mode speaks the steps. Go, slow, and stop are lights, not a speed number. The sound stays quiet when the train is still.

Still in beta. We’re testing it with kids.
